Brand assets used by Joboard:

- Claude: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pheralb/svgl/main/static/library/claude-ai-icon.svg
- OpenAI / ChatGPT: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pheralb/svgl/main/static/library/openai.svg
  (viewBox cropped to the mark; paths unchanged).
- SVGL library: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl (MIT).
- Company icons: Google favicon service for the domains recorded in
  `company-logos.json`. Domain discovery uses exact-name matches from
  Clearbit autocomplete and matching website links already in the tracker.
  The manifest, the cached icons (`companies/`) and the optional
  `tools/company-aliases.json` are generated locally by
  `tools/cache_company_logos.py` and are not published.

Company marks belong to their respective owners. Missing or ambiguous marks
use initials. No application records or conversation content are embedded
in the asset manifest.
